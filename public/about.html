About Aman Pharma

15+ years of dedication to India's healthcare landscape

Our Story

Building Trust Since 2010

Aman Pharma was founded with a simple vision: make quality healthcare accessible and affordable for every Indian. What started as a small pharmacy in Mumbai has grown into a nationally recognized pharmaceutical brand.

Our state-of-the-art manufacturing facility spans 50,000 sq ft and is equipped with the latest technology. Every product goes through rigorous quality checks before reaching our customers.

With a team of 200+ pharmaceutical experts, doctors, and support staff, we serve over 50,000 customers across 200+ cities in India.
